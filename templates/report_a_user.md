## Your problem
Reading long ticket threads (10+ messages) before you can answer takes too much time.

## Fix it today with Ticket Lens (internal)
Ticket Lens is already in your CS platform. It is just switched off by default.

1. Open any ticket → **Apps** panel (right) → **Ticket Lens** → **Enable** → **Pin to sidebar**.
2. Click **Summarise** before reading: you get issue, what was tried and what the user wants in 3 lines.
3. Click **Suggest macro** to get the 3 best-fitting replies out of 300+.
4. If the category is wrong, correct it and click 👎. This trains the model.
5. Edit and send the reply yourself.

**Try this:** click Summarise, then add *"Add the last promise we made to the user."*
